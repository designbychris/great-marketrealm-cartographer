# VI.6D.10B — Pippin Plants His First Five Trees

Adds four camera-specific PNG sprites for MarketRealm Oak, Whispering Pine, Fructan Orchard, Goldenleaf and Riverside Willow. Each is transparent 192×340 native pixels, rendered at 32×57 SVG units. Stable cell-based selection is inherited from VI.6D.10A. Classic illustration retains its existing trees. The scene document and its geometry are unchanged. Future collection packs can use the existing registry contract.

Smoke-test all four orientations, Classic vs Pixel, depth guides, pan/zoom, and save/reload. The sprites are stylised first-pass pixel illustrations, not final bespoke hand-painted environmental art.
