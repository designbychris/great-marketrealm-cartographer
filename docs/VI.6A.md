# VI.6A — Pippin Paints the First Masterpiece

A non-destructive, deterministic illustration pass on the existing SVG isometric renderer.

- Terrain variation and small meadow details are calculated from tile coordinates, not stored.
- Water gains a second ripple detail; stone walls and trees gain highlights.
- Building fronts receive windows and doors; cottages, taverns and blacksmiths receive chimney details.
- Existing scene storage, placement, collisions, prefab catalogue, and four camera directions remain unchanged.
- This is procedural illustration, not yet the final illustrated art-pack system. Further phases will introduce authored assets and depth-aware compositing.

Check all four camera views, save and refresh an existing scene, and verify unchanged terrain and object positions.
