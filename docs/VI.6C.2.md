# VI.6C.2 — Pippin Gives Every Building a Face

Presentation-only architectural details layered onto the existing VI.6C.1 sketchbook:

- Cottage thatch strokes and timber posts; tavern terracotta seams and framing.
- Slate roof seams and sparse stonework on blacksmiths and watchtowers.
- Canvas stripe details for market roofs; refined door lintels and window muntins/sills.
- A reinforced blacksmith chimney with a small static smoke flourish.
- Roof and wall detail lines are projected from world-space coordinates for the four camera directions.
- Existing roof/base/details depth ordering, footprint, object rotation, zoom, drag camera and scene persistence are unchanged.

Test all four camera angles, including depth guides, and check roof strokes at 150–200% zoom. These are deliberately lightweight procedural details, not a new scene schema or selectable building components.
