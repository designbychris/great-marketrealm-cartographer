# VI.6B.9 — Pippin Takes a Closer Look

View-only zoom (50–200%) for the illustrated landscape. Mouse wheel zooms towards the cursor, plus/minus buttons adjust by 25%, Reset view restores 100% and centre. Shift+drag pans when zoomed in; ordinary horizontal drag continues to rotate the four-view camera. Zoom/pan do not alter saved draft objects or terrain. Zoom is intentionally not persisted. On touch devices use zoom buttons; touch swipes remain assigned to rotation.
